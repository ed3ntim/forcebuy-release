The service

ForceBuy keeps a ledger of CS2 items: what you own, what you paid and what it is worth. During the closed test it is free. There is a website at forcebuy.net and a local app that keeps its data on your machine.

The website is open to accounts on the allowlist. Sign-in is through Steam. ForceBuy never asks for a Steam password. The local app has no account of its own.

What ForceBuy will not do

Calls to Steam and to marketplaces are read-only. ForceBuy does not list, buy, sell, trade or change a price on your behalf. The one write to a service you control is a backup to your own Google Drive, and only if you turn that on in the local app.

Sessions and keys you store

A session cookie is the whole session of that site, not a read-only permission. Storing one so that ForceBuy can read your inventory or your history can be against that site's terms and can lead to restrictions on the account, up to a ban. You do not have to store one. The ledger works without it, with whatever that site publishes without a sign-in.

What you do store is kept encrypted, is never shown again, and is used only to read. You can remove it in the settings. Without a sign-in it expires after 30 days.
