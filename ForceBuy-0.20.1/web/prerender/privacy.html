What ForceBuy does with your data

ForceBuy keeps a ledger of your CS2 items: what you own, what you paid and what it is worth today. For that it processes your Steam account, your inventory and, if you allow it, your buying and selling history. The legal basis is performing the service you signed up for (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR) and, wherever you are asked explicitly, your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR).

What is stored

  • Your Steam account: SteamID64, public display name, avatar address. They come from your Steam sign-in; ForceBuy never sees a password.
  • Your ledger: items, purchase and sale prices, dates, your own notes, collections, settings and the figures worked out from them.
  • Access you store: a Steam session, marketplace API keys. They are kept encrypted, never shown again, and used only for the requests you ask for. Every use is logged and visible in your settings. You can revoke them at any time, and without a sign-in they expire by themselves after 30 days.
  • Sessions: a cookie keeps you signed in. Only a checksum of it is stored, with the time and the browser string of the sign-in.
  • Logs: the server keeps technical logs (time, path, errors) to run the service. Cloudflare collects aggregate traffic figures.

Prices, the item catalogue and exchange rates describe items, not people. They are stored once for everyone and stay when an account is deleted.

No tracking

No advertising, no analytics services, no recognition across sites. The cookies that are set are the ones the service needs: your sign-in and the access code of the closed test. Fonts and scripts come from ForceBuy itself; only item images load from Steam's image servers.

Who else sees it

  • Hetzner Online GmbH (Nuremberg, Germany): server and database, as a processor.
  • Cloudflare: delivery, protection of the domain and the connection to the server. Data may be processed outside the EU in the course of this.
  • Steam (Valve), CSFloat, Skinport, Buff163: sources for inventory, history and prices. Requests go to them either because you asked for them or because they run once for everybody. ForceBuy only ever reads from these services.

How long

Your account and your ledger stay until you delete them. Stored access expires after 30 days without a sign-in. Technical logs are overwritten after a short time.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, deletion, restriction and portability, and withdraw a consent at any time. Deleting your account with everything in it is a button in your settings; it happens at once and cannot be undone. For anything else, a message to contact@forcebuy.net is enough.

You can also complain to a supervisory authority; for the controller that is the Unabhängiges Datenschutzzentrum Saarland.

ForceBuy is not affiliated with Valve, Steam or the marketplaces named here.
