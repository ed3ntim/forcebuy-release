ForceBuy

Every CS2 item you own, what it cost, and what it is worth.

ForceBuy reads your Steam inventory and your purchases on the Steam Market and the marketplaces you use, and keeps them in one ledger: value, cost basis, profit and loss, in your currency.

Sign in through

ForceBuy on the web is in a closed test. Signing in shows whether your account is part of it.

Invented items and prices, no Steam account, gone after two hours.

Real purchase prices, automatically

Every purchase and sale on the Steam Market, CSFloat and Buff163 is read and tied to its item, so each one keeps what you paid for it. No spreadsheet, nothing to type in.

All marketplaces in one view

Prices from Steam, CSFloat, Buff163, YouPin898, Skinport and C5Game side by side, and dashboards you lay out yourself.

Every copy valued as what it is

Float and pattern on every item, applied stickers that can add to its value, Doppler phases priced on their own, and what a sale would leave after each marketplace's fees.

Storage units and past items count too

Items in storage units are read and valued like the rest, and items that already left the account keep their record: what they cost, what they sold for, what they made.

Rather keep it on your own computer?

The local app for Windows does the same with your data, your keys and your Steam session in one folder on your PC.
